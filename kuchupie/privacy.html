Privacy Policy

Kuchupie · Last updated November 10, 2026

Short version: Kuchupie does not collect any of your data. Everything stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Your game progress (levels, stars, XP, gems, streaks, achievements, shop items), any puzzle in progress, and your settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No internet connection is needed or used.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, Kuchupie schedules local notifications on your device. They are created and delivered entirely on your iPhone; no server is involved. You can turn them off in the app (Me → Settings) or in iPhone Settings at any time.

Children

Kuchupie is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Me → Reset all progress in the app, or delete the app. Both remove all data the app has stored.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions? Email vasajitha@gmail.com.
