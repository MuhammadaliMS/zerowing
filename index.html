Principles

What we believe in

Every product we ship is guided by a set of non-negotiable principles.

01

Radical simplicity

We strip away everything that doesn't serve the user. Every feature earns its place. No bloat, no clutter.

02

Privacy by default

We don't track you. We don't collect your data. No accounts required, no analytics watching.

03

Free forever

No paywalls, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. Great tools should be accessible to everyone.

04

Real utility

If it doesn't solve a real problem, we don't ship it. Every product starts with a need, not a trend.

05

Craft over speed

We take time to get details right. Smooth animations, thoughtful layouts, considered interactions.

06

User-first always

No dark patterns. No manipulative design. The user's experience comes before everything else.

Process

How we work

From idea to launch, we keep things lean and focused.

Identify

Find a real problem worth solving. If it's not a genuine pain point, we move on.

Design

Minimal, intuitive interfaces. No learning curve. Open the app, get things done.

Build

Clean code, fast performance, small footprint. We obsess over the details.

Ship

Launch quickly, iterate based on real feedback. No feature creep, no scope bloat.
